# Mike Automations

Workflow automation, custom internal systems and AI agents for small service businesses.

Mike Automations is one person, Michael Gershman. I map how a small business works, then build and support the
automations that take the routine admin off it: chasing clients for documents, reminders, onboarding, and follow-ups
on quotes and invoices. It's built on the tools the business already uses. A person always approves anything AI
prepares.

**Who it's for:** small service businesses such as accounting and bookkeeping firms, agencies, trades, letting agents
and law firms, in the UK, US, Ireland, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

**The offer:** one workflow automation for a fixed $2,000 ($1,500 for the first 3 clients), live in 2-3 days, and you
pay only once it's running and you're happy with it. Custom systems from $5,000, optional maintenance $150/month.
